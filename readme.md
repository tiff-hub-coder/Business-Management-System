# Business Management & Analytics System

    A Python-based Business Management & Analytics System designed to help consultants and business owners manage multiple businesses from a single application.

    This project is being developed as a long-term portfolio project while learning Python, SQL, data analytics, and business intelligence.

    ---

    ##  Project Goals

        The aim of this application is to:

        - Manage multiple business clients
        - Track customers, employees and suppliers
        - Manage products and services
        - Record revenue and expenses
        - Generate invoices and receipts
        - Produce financial reports
        - Analyse business performance
        - Generate business insights and recommendations
